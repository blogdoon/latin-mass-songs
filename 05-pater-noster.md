# Pater Noster — Our Father

|        | Latina                                                                       | Pronunciation                                                                                   | English                                                               |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Priest | Praeceptis salutaribus moniti, et divina institutione formati, audemus dicere: | preh-CHEHP-tees sah-loo-TAH-ree-boos MOH-nee-tee, eht dee-VEE-nah een-stee-too-tsee-OH-neh fohr-MAH-tee, au-DEH-moos DEE-cheh-reh | Guided by saving teaching and formed by God's instruction, we dare to say: |
| All    | Pater noster, qui es in caelis:                                              | PAH-tehr NOH-stehr, kwee ehs een CHEH-lees                                                      | Our Father, who are in heaven,                                        |
| All    | sanctificetur nomen tuum;                                                    | sahnk-tee-fee-CHEH-toor NOH-mehn TOO-oom                                                        | may your name be held holy;                                           |
| All    | adveniat regnum tuum;                                                        | ahd-VEH-nee-aht REH-nyoom TOO-oom                                                               | may your kingdom come;                                                |
| All    | fiat voluntas tua, sicut in caelo, et in terra.                              | FEE-aht voh-LOON-tahs TOO-ah, SEE-koot een CHEH-loh, eht een TEHR-rah                            | may your will be done, as in heaven, so on earth.                     |
| All    | Panem nostrum cotidianum da nobis hodie;                                     | PAH-nehm NOH-stroom koh-tee-dee-AH-noom dah NOH-bees OH-dee-eh                                   | Give us today our daily bread;                                        |
| All    | et dimitte nobis debita nostra,                                              | eht dee-MEET-teh NOH-bees DEH-bee-tah NOH-strah                                                 | and forgive us our debts,                                             |
| All    | sicut et nos dimittimus debitoribus nostris;                                 | SEE-koot eht nohs dee-MEET-tee-moos deh-bee-TOH-ree-boos NOH-strees                             | as we also forgive our debtors;                                       |
| All    | et ne nos inducas in temptationem;                                           | eht neh nohs een-DOO-kahs een tehmp-tah-tsee-OH-nehm                                            | and do not lead us into temptation;                                   |
| All    | sed libera nos a malo.                                                       | sehd LEE-beh-rah nohs ah MAH-loh                                                                | but deliver us from evil.                                             |
| All    | Quia tuum est regnum, et potestas, et gloria in saecula.                     | KWEE-ah TOO-oom ehst REH-nyoom, eht poh-TEH-stahs, eht GLOH-ree-ah een SEH-koo-lah               | (After the priest's prayer) For yours is the kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever. |
