# Credo in Unum Deum — I Believe in One God

|        | Latina                                                | Pronunciation                                                        | English                                                    |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Priest | Credo in unum Deum,                                   | KREH-doh een OO-noom DEH-oom                                         | I believe in one God,                                      |
| All    | Patrem omnipotentem,                                  | PAH-trehm ohm-nee-poh-TEHN-tehm                                      | the Father almighty,                                       |
| All    | factorem caeli et terrae,                             | fahk-TOH-rehm CHEH-lee eht TEHR-reh                                  | maker of heaven and earth,                                 |
| All    | visibilium omnium et invisibilium.                    | vee-see-BEE-lee-oom OHM-nee-oom eht een-vee-see-BEE-lee-oom          | of all things visible and invisible.                       |
| All    | Et in unum Dominum Iesum Christum,                    | eht een OO-noom DOH-mee-noom YEH-soom KREE-stoom                     | And in one Lord, Jesus Christ,                             |
| All    | Filium Dei unigenitum,                                | FEE-lee-oom DEH-ee oo-nee-JEH-nee-toom                               | the only-begotten Son of God,                              |
| All    | et ex Patre natum ante omnia saecula.                 | eht ehks PAH-treh NAH-toom AHN-teh OHM-nee-ah SEH-koo-lah            | born of the Father before all ages.                        |
| All    | Deum de Deo, lumen de lumine,                         | DEH-oom deh DEH-oh, LOO-mehn deh LOO-mee-neh                         | God from God, light from light,                            |
| All    | Deum verum de Deo vero,                               | DEH-oom VEH-room deh DEH-oh VEH-roh                                  | true God from true God,                                    |
| All    | genitum, non factum,                                  | JEH-nee-toom, nohn FAHK-toom                                         | begotten, not made,                                        |
| All    | consubstantialem Patri:                               | kohn-soob-stahn-tsee-AH-lehm PAH-tree                                | of one substance with the Father;                          |
| All    | per quem omnia facta sunt.                            | pehr kwehm OHM-nee-ah FAHK-tah soont                                 | through him all things were made.                          |
| All    | Qui propter nos homines                               | kwee PROHP-tehr nohs OH-mee-nehs                                     | For us men                                                 |
| All    | et propter nostram salutem                            | eht PROHP-tehr NOH-strahm sah-LOO-tehm                               | and for our salvation                                      |
| All    | descendit de caelis.                                  | deh-SHEHN-deet deh CHEH-lees                                         | he came down from heaven.                                  |
| All    | Et incarnatus est de Spiritu Sancto                   | eht een-kahr-NAH-toos ehst deh SPEE-ree-too SAHNK-toh                | And he took flesh by the Holy Spirit                       |
| All    | ex Maria Virgine, et homo factus est.                 | ehks mah-REE-ah VEER-jee-neh, eht OH-moh FAHK-toos ehst              | from the Virgin Mary, and became man.                      |
| All    | Crucifixus etiam pro nobis sub Pontio Pilato;         | kroo-chee-FEEK-soos EH-tsee-ahm proh NOH-bees soob POHN-tsee-oh pee-LAH-toh | He was crucified for us under Pontius Pilate;       |
| All    | passus et sepultus est,                               | PAHS-soos eht seh-POOL-toos ehst                                     | he suffered and was buried,                                |
| All    | et resurrexit tertia die, secundum Scripturas,        | eht reh-soor-REHK-seet TEHR-tsee-ah DEE-eh, seh-KOON-doom skreep-TOO-rahs | and rose again on the third day, as the Scriptures say, |
| All    | et ascendit in caelum,                                | eht ah-SHEHN-deet een CHEH-loom                                      | and went up into heaven,                                   |
| All    | sedet ad dexteram Patris.                             | SEH-deht ahd DEHK-steh-rahm PAH-trees                                | and sits at the right hand of the Father.                  |
| All    | Et iterum venturus est cum gloria,                    | eht EE-teh-room vehn-TOO-roos ehst koom GLOH-ree-ah                  | He will come again in glory                                |
| All    | iudicare vivos et mortuos,                            | yoo-dee-KAH-reh VEE-vohs eht MOHR-too-ohs                            | to judge the living and the dead,                          |
| All    | cuius regni non erit finis.                           | KOO-yoos REH-nyee nohn EH-reet FEE-nees                              | and his kingdom will have no end.                          |
| All    | Et in Spiritum Sanctum, Dominum et vivificantem:      | eht een SPEE-ree-toom SAHNK-toom, DOH-mee-noom eht vee-vee-fee-KAHN-tehm | And in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the giver of life,   |
| All    | qui ex Patre Filioque procedit.                       | kwee ehks PAH-treh fee-lee-OH-kweh proh-CHEH-deet                    | who comes from the Father and the Son.                     |
| All    | Qui cum Patre et Filio simul adoratur                 | kwee koom PAH-treh eht FEE-lee-oh SEE-mool ah-doh-RAH-toor           | With the Father and the Son he is adored                   |
| All    | et conglorificatur:                                   | eht kohn-gloh-ree-fee-KAH-toor                                       | and glorified together;                                    |
| All    | qui locutus est per prophetas.                        | kwee loh-KOO-toos ehst pehr proh-FEH-tahs                            | he has spoken through the prophets.                        |
| All    | Et unam, sanctam, catholicam et apostolicam Ecclesiam. | eht OO-nahm, SAHNK-tahm, kah-TOH-lee-kahm eht ah-poh-STOH-lee-kahm ehk-KLEH-see-ahm | And in one, holy, catholic and apostolic Church. |
| All    | Confiteor unum baptisma in remissionem peccatorum.    | kohn-FEE-teh-ohr OO-noom bahp-TEEZ-mah een reh-mees-see-OH-nehm pehk-kah-TOH-room | I confess one baptism for the forgiveness of sins. |
| All    | Et exspecto resurrectionem mortuorum,                 | eht ehk-SPEHK-toh reh-soor-rehk-tsee-OH-nehm mohr-too-OH-room        | And I look forward to the resurrection of the dead,        |
| All    | et vitam venturi saeculi. Amen.                       | eht VEE-tahm vehn-TOO-ree SEH-koo-lee. AH-mehn                       | and the life of the world to come. Amen.                   |
